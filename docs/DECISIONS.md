# Decisions log

Judgement calls made while building, per `CLAUDE.md`. Format: date · decision · why.

| Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-21 | **One client shell for `/s/*`** (`app/s/[[...path]]`), with views switched by `history.pushState` instead of App Router navigation. | App Router fetches RSC payloads from the server on navigation, which fails offline. Once the shell is cached, profile, serve and pending views work with no network. A cold deep link opened offline falls back to the cached `/s` shell. |
| 2026-11-21 | **`lead_pin_hash` column added to `stations`** (not in DataModel). The lead PIN gives `station_lead` at join, and approves food-duplicate overrides via `request_lead_override` → one-shot `lead_overrides` row. | DataModel needed a way to make someone a Station Lead and to validate overrides. The confidential `role_pin` stays separate so medical/counselling access is still deliberate. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Offline food override** is accepted as `override_by = "offline:<lead name> via <volunteer>"` and raises a `leads` flag for review. | Bcrypt PIN hashes must never be sent to devices (6-digit PINs are brute-forceable), so a lead PIN can't be checked offline. The duplicate guard protects fairness, not data, so allowing the override with a visible flag is an acceptable trade. |
| 2026-11-21 | **`station_secrets` table** (service-role only, AES-256-GCM with a key derived from `JOIN_TOKEN_SECRET`) stores a recoverable copy of PINs. | Posters must print the PIN, but `stations` only holds bcrypt hashes. Training PINs are stored as `training:<pin>` and only decrypt for `is_training` editions. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Join links** use random tokens whose SHA-256 is stored in `station_join_tokens`, not HMAC-signed JWTs. | Revocable, no shared secret in Postgres, and verification lives entirely in `join_station_token`. |
| 2026-11-21 | `edition_id` **denormalised onto `visit_needs` and `flags`**. | Supabase Realtime `postgres_changes` can only filter on a column of the changed table (`edition_id=eq.<id>`). |
| 2026-11-21 | **Need states:** `referred` means "pending because another station referred them". Pending lists show `pending` + `referred`. A referral never downgrades `pending`; it does reopen a `served` need. | Keeps the PRD's Pending / Served / Declined / Referred chips without a separate queue table. |
| 2026-11-21 | **RLS for idempotent upserts:** registration sessions may read visit-less beneficiaries of their org; sessions may read flags they created. (migration `…006`) | `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` applies SELECT policies to the proposed row. Without this, offline registrations could never sync. Covered by `tests/03_upsert_sync.sql`. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Card conflict on sync** (two offline devices used the same card): the visit is re-sent without a card code and a `card_conflict` flag goes to leads. | No lost registrations. The coordinator sees it in the data-quality alerts and hands out a new card. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Offline snapshot holds `phone_last4`, not full phone numbers.** | Data minimisation on lost devices. Full phones are only on devices that registered that person, and in the admin console. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Stock may go negative** when offline devices overdraw. | Recording what was really given matters more than blocking. The dashboard shows negative stock in red. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Confidential views by station sessions are not audited per read; admin reads (break-glass) are.** | Station staff read their own notes constantly, so one audit row per glance would swamp the log. Break-glass and `get_confidential` by admins are always logged. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Exports never include confidential payloads**, even for super admins. Phone numbers appear only for people with follow-up consent. | NDPA minimisation. Break-glass, per person and time-boxed, covers the rare legitimate need. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Fuse search runs on the main thread**, not in a Web Worker. | For ≤ 5,000 people, index build and search measure well under the 50 ms budget. A worker would add complexity and bundle weight. Revisit if editions grow. |
| 2026-11-21 | **supabase-js is lazily imported in the volunteer app.** | With secondary views also lazy-loaded, `/s` initial JS is 189.5 KB gzip (budget 200 KB). The shell renders from IndexedDB first. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Dashboard KPIs are computed on demand** by `dashboard_stats` (5 s polling plus realtime nudges), not a materialised view. | Well under 100 ms on 5k people / 30k services. Simpler, and always matches ground truth. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Report route is `/api/reports/summary`** (no `.pdf` suffix); exports accept `?format=xlsx|csv`. | Next.js route segments with dots complicate dynamic params. The content type and filename are still PDF/XLSX. |
| 2026-11-21 | **i18n:** UI strings are English and inline in components for v1. | The PRD lists English for v1. Extracting to `src/lib/i18n/en.json` is a mechanical follow-up before adding Pidgin/Hausa. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Open PRD questions (date, turnout, devices, checkout station, SMS)** were answered with defaults: draft edition "Gratitude Outreach 2026" on 12 Dec 2026, checkout station kind is supported but not created by default, no SMS. | None block the build. Confirm with the outreach team. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Primary buttons and the scan button use `leaf-600` (#136F0E) instead of `leaf` (#188B12).** | White on `leaf` measures 4.42:1, just under WCAG AA 4.5:1 (axe flagged it). DesignSystem already recommends `leaf-600` for buttons, and it reads better in sunlight. `leaf` stays for accents, bars and borders. |
| 2026-11-21 | **PIN lockout: 5 failures per phone, 30 per IP** (5-minute window), not 5 per IP. | Phones at the venue share one public IP. 5 per IP would let a few honest typos lock out every volunteer. 30 still makes brute force useless against 1,000,000 PINs. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Each phone pulls a delta every 30 s** with the heartbeat, in addition to Realtime. | The load test showed Supabase Realtime dropping fan-out under bursts. The delta guarantees every phone catches up, for one small request per phone every 30 s. |
| 2026-11-21 | **After a session ends, the local database is locked for that page** (`SessionEndedError`) before it is deleted. | The revoke test showed an in-flight sync could re-create data on a wiped phone. Locking makes the purge final. |
| 2026-11-21 | **Text sizes are `rem`, not `px`.** | Type then scales with the phone's font-size setting (NFR: works with large system fonts). |
| 2026-11-21 | **Error monitoring is a scrubbed beacon to `/api/client-errors` (server logs), not Sentry.** | No extra vendor holding data during an NDPA-sensitive event. Sentry can be wired into one function later. |
| 2026-11-22 | **Registration prints a QR slip by default; pre-printed cards are optional.** Codes come from per-phone blocks (`GO26-R<block>-NNN`, 999 per block), with the next block fetched in the background below 150 left (migration `…008`, max 20 blocks per session). | The outreach team asked for a QR printed at the stand. Blocks keep codes unique across phones while offline. The QR is generated on the phone and printed with the browser's print dialog (AirPrint, Android print services, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth printers), so no printer SDK is needed. |
| 2026-11-22 | **The slip shows the first name**, not just the code. The QR payload is still only the code. | Helps volunteers confirm they have the right person. A first name on paper the person holds is low risk; no surname, phone or needs are printed. |
| 2026-11-22 | **Stations tick a short checklist after scanning**; the detailed forms stay behind "More details". Ticks are saved in the existing service-record shapes (`outcomes` added). | The team wants volunteers to tick what was done, not fill in forms. Reusing the shapes keeps follow-ups (salvation, wants follow-up), the food duplicate guard, the dashboard and exports working. |
| 2026-11-22 | **Quick medical ticks go into the confidential record**; the timeline summary stays "Medical check" (or "· referred"). | "Given medication" is medical detail (DataModel §3). |
| 2026-11-22 | **Food and clothing: one "received" tick; which stock items were given is optional.** `items` may be empty. | Keeps the tick to one tap. Stock still decrements when items are picked. |
| 2026-11-22 | **Admin dashboard shows a live activity feed** (registrations and station ticks with names), for coordinators and super admins only. | The Super Admin wants to watch registrations and updates as they happen. Viewers still only see counts. |
| 2026-11-23 | **Prayer and Counselling are one station ("Prayer & Counselling") by default.** Its checklist adds *Has been counselled*. The confidential Counselling station kind is kept but switched off, so a team that needs private counselling notes can turn it back on. | The outreach team asked to combine them. Keeping the kind avoids deleting history and keeps the confidential path tested. |
| 2026-11-23 | **One code sequence per edition** (`GO26-0001`…) shared by pre-printed cards and phone-printed QR slips. Phones get blocks of 200 plain numbers from `app.claim_code_numbers`, which also feeds `generate_cards`, under one per-edition lock. | People should only need to remember the number. Gaps from unused blocks are fine; collisions are not. |
| 2026-11-23 | **New Pharmacy station kind** (role PIN like Medical). Medical writes the prescription into the medical confidential record and refers the person to Pharmacy; Pharmacy may read *medical* confidential records (not counselling) to see it, and dispenses with `data.items`, which reduces stock. | Prescriptions are medical detail (DataModel §3), and pharmacists are clinical staff. Reusing the medical confidential record avoids a new table and new sync path; the timeline only says "Medical check" / "Medicine given". |
| 2026-11-23 | **Medicines are any inventory items under the Medical or Pharmacy station.** The old quick tick "Given medication" is replaced by the prescription. | Drugs were already registered under Medical; the coordinator can also add them under Pharmacy. |
| 2026-11-23 | **Volunteer accounts** (`volunteer_accounts`, migration `…011`). Registering needs the station PIN or join QR, a name and a phone number. Signing in needs the same name and phone number (the phone is the password, name matched ignoring case and extra spaces). One account can be signed in on many devices; each device still has its own session, so a coordinator can end one phone without ending the others. Moving a volunteer moves the account and all its devices. A wrong name or phone counts as a failed attempt under the existing lockout. | The outreach team wanted volunteers to have accounts and use several devices. A phone number is not secret, so registering still needs the station PIN (it proves the person is at the event), and Medical, Pharmacy and Counselling still ask for the role PIN and the confidentiality agreement on every device. The lead PIN is also per device: signing in never makes someone a Station Lead. |
| 2026-11-25 | **The Cause a Smile website lives in this project**: `/` is the NGO home, `/gratitudeoutreach` the public outreach page, `/gratitudeoutreach/team` the old launcher. `/s`, `/join` and `/admin` do not move; `/gratitudeoutreach/join` and `/gratitudeoutreach/admin` redirect to them. | One deployment and domain. A base path would change the service worker scope, installed PWAs, stored offline data paths and posters just before the event. |
| 2026-11-25 | **Public pages are indexable and don't link the app manifest**; everything else stays `noindex`. The manifest is now a route handler. | Visitors should find the NGO on Google and should not be prompted to install the volunteer app. |
| 2026-11-25 | **Contact, event date and venue live in `src/lib/site.ts` and are hidden while `null`.** | No confirmed details yet; the 12 Dec date is only a build default. |
| 2026-11-25 | **Patient flow from Pastor Ade:** Registration → Medical Assistant → General Doctor (→ Optician / Laboratory) → Counselling → Pharmacy → Clothing and Food. Medical Assistant, Counselling and Pharmacy needs are added for everyone by a trigger on `visits` (and locally on the phone). Migrations `…012`, `…013`. | The doctor is seen only if needed; Pharmacy has "No medicine needed". Other steps warn but do not block, so a queue never stops food. |
| 2026-11-25 | **Pharmacy gate is enforced in the database** (`trg_pharmacy_gate`) and on the phone. Override: lead PIN online, or lead name offline (accepted and flagged), as for food. A record that cites a counselling record undone later is still accepted. | Offline phones may not know Counselling is done. What was dispensed must never be lost. |
| 2026-11-25 | **The station kind `prayer` is now "Counselling".** Its shared record is only `{counselled: true}`; outcomes go to a new confidential kind `spiritual`, readable by Counselling sessions and coordinators without break-glass. Faith adds "Neither". | Keeps history and PINs. Faith and healing are sensitive data that Food or Pharmacy do not need. Coordinators need them to report. |
| 2026-11-25 | **New kinds `medical_assistant`, `optician`, `laboratory` share the medical confidential record** with General Doctor and Pharmacy. Lab and Optician needs, records and alerts are visible to the medical team and coordinators only; tests and private referrals live in the confidential payload and are applied by a trigger. Minor surgery is a field plus a medical-only alert, not a station. | Being sent to the lab is itself medical detail. One record means no re-entry. |
| 2026-11-25 | **The doctor's form no longer dispenses**, and the timeline for Pharmacy always says "Pharmacy completed". | Dispensing at Medical would bypass the gate. |
| 2026-11-25 | **Moving a phone to a different station type clears private notes** and reloads what the new station may see. | Notes from the old station stayed on the phone. |
| 2026-11-25 | **Public pages follow the layout style of a paid theme (Givo) the owner chose**, built from scratch with our tokens, copy and motif. Donate and contact slots stay empty until supplied. | The owner wants that look; the theme's code, photos and text are licensed, and NGO facts must not be invented. Beneficiary photos need permission (DesignSystem §7). |
| 2026-11-25 | **Public pages use photos of people**, an exception to DesignSystem §7 at the owner's request: one from the last event, the rest illustrative photos of Africans from Wikimedia Commons with licence credits in the footer. NGO pages use the NGO logo colours (`theme-smile`). | Owner's choice. Photos with other organisations' branding were left out so no partnership is implied. |
